Discover Human Proteins

Proteins are the molecular machines of life — they build, repair, and regulate everything in your body.

  • 👉 Hemoglobin carries oxygen in your blood.
  • 👉 Collagen gives strength and elasticity to your skin and tissues.
  • 👉 Enzymes speed up chemical reactions that keep you alive.
  • 👉 Antibodies are proteins that defend you against infections.
  • 👉 Even your hair and nails are made of protein (keratin).
  • 👉 Creatine enhances high-intensity exercise performance, boosts strength, aids muscle recovery, and can increase muscle mass
