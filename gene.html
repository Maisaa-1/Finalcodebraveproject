Gene Information

Fun Gene Facts

Humans have about 20,000–25,000 genes.
Genes are made of DNA, which acts like a recipe book for proteins.
The TP53 gene is nicknamed the “guardian of the genome.”
Only about 2% of your DNA actually codes for proteins — the rest has regulatory or unknown roles.
Identical twins have the same genes, but their gene expression can differ due to environment.
Mitochondria have their own DNA, separate from the DNA in the cell nucleus.
Some genes can be turned on or off depending on diet, stress, or lifestyle — this is called epigenetics.
The longest human gene is DMD, which spans over 2.2 million base pairs.
Genes can “jump” — transposons are DNA sequences that move around the genome.
Your eye color is influenced by multiple genes, not just one.
